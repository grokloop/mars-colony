Base Alpha · Arcadia Planitia

A living Mars settlement.

Not a game. No scores, quests, or win state. This is a public, walkable first-outpost that follows the Musk / SpaceX civilize-Mars plan — and it grows as people send pull requests.

Starship is the truck. Uncrewed cargo lands first, proves it lands intact, and delivers surface systems. First human jobs are survey, prepare landing surfaces, set up solar power, and build habitats. Ice is mined for water. ISRU is Sabatier: Mars CO2 + water ice → CH4 and O2. A city comes later, not now.

Outpost snapshot

What is on the ground today. The world updates over time — this list is a briefing, not a scoreboard.

  • Six Starships across two synodic windows. The pad ship is fitted as the first crew hab, with an EVA suit rack by the airlock. Five cargo landings; three are unloading.
  • Next-window pad graded, marked, and being sintered against plume erosion — still empty, waiting on the next 26-month window.
  • Power from four solar fields east of the pad, a dust fence and combiner on field 4, with battery yards plus a third hab night store so the plant and crew run after dark.
  • Sabatier ISRU west of the pad, now two trains plus a radiator bank, a CH4 liquefaction chiller, a second CO2 intake, and a water electrolysis / H2 recycle skid, ice/water feed, CH4 and O2 tanks, methalox lines through a transfer pump house to the spaceport stand, a load arm toward the pad ship, a visible return-flight stockpile, and CH4 / O2 return buffers.
  • Hab street HAB 2–6 linked by pressurized corridors. HAB 6 is occupied. Crew ops / medical and a cargo-built commons / mess are tubed into the neighborhood. Workshop bays sit east of the yard, with an open rover dust shelter, a spare-parts cargo depot, and a sinter press.
  • Life support — two CO2 greenhouses with a shared dust filter, two more grow vaults, a hab ECLSS / CO2 scrub skid, a waste / water-recovery skid, a crew O2 buffer, a visible water loop from two ice mines, an ice crush / feed depot with a parked ice-haul rover, an ice-melt still at mine 1, a hab condensate return, and an ice/cargo haul road.
  • Survey and comms on the ice-rich northern plain, a hab-street weather / dust mast, plus two Earth-pointing dishes (light-time delay, not real-time; one is a backup).

See it. Walk it.

Live preview is a cinematic spectator of the current world. No controls — the camera tours the pad, habs, ISRU, and the empty next-window deck.

Walk is first-person on the surface. Click to lock the pointer, then WASD, mouse look, Shift to run, Space to jump. Spawn is on the landing-pad berm, looking at the Starships.

A world that changes

This is not a frozen screenshot. Structures, colliders, and landmarks land through ordinary git history. If you want a new hab, a less-janky Starship, or a better rover, open a PR. People walking tomorrow will see it.

Stay on the plan: cargo first, solar, ice, Sabatier, landing-surface prep, then habs. No backends, accounts, or analytics.

Clone and contribute

cd mars-colony
npm install
npm run dev
