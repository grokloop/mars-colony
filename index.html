Base Alpha · Arcadia Planitia

A living Mars settlement.

Not a game. No scores, quests, or win state. This is a public, walkable first-outpost that follows the Musk / SpaceX civilize-Mars plan — and it grows as people send pull requests.

Starship is the truck. Uncrewed cargo lands first, proves it lands intact, and delivers surface systems. First human jobs are survey, prepare landing surfaces, set up solar power, and build habitats. Ice is mined for water. ISRU is Sabatier: Mars CO2 + water ice → CH4 and O2. A city comes later, not now.

Outpost snapshot

What is on the ground today. The world updates over time — this list is a briefing, not a scoreboard.

  • Six Starships across two synodic windows. The pad ship is fitted as the first crew hab, with an EVA suit rack by the airlock and approach lights, west-pad floods, and a sinter roller on the next-window pad. Five cargo landings; three are unloading, with a crane on the window-2 ship.
  • Next-window pad graded, marked, sintered against plume erosion, and ringed with landing beacons — still empty, waiting on the next 26-month window.
  • Power from four solar fields east of the pad, with night lighting and a spare inverter on field 4, a combiner, night store, and dust fence on each field, a buried power trench to the pad batteries and a hab-street trench between night stores 3 and 4, with battery yards plus a fourth hab night store and a comms night pack at the Earth dishes so the plant, crew, and Earth link run after dark.
  • Sabatier ISRU west of the pad, now two trains plus radiator banks on both, water-knockout condensers on both trains, CH4 and O2 chillers on both trains, CO2 intakes on both trains, electrolysis skids and H2 recycle tanks on both trains, process-water tanks on both ice-to-ISRU lines, ice/water feed, CH4 and O2 tanks, methalox lines through a transfer pump house to the spaceport stand, a load arm toward the pad ship, a visible return-flight stockpile with a farm berm, night lighting at the plant and the methalox farm, and CH4 / O2 return buffers.
  • Hab street HAB 2–6 linked by pressurized corridors, with an EVA dust lock, a suit locker, and night lighting on hab street. HAB 6 is occupied. Crew ops / medical and a cargo-built commons / mess are tubed into the neighborhood. Three workshop bays sit east of the yard, with an open rover dust shelter, a spare-parts cargo depot, a sinter press, a sinter kiln, a brick pallet, a tool crib, a parts rack, workshop night lights, and a regolith stockpile for local sinter feed.
  • Life support — two CO2 greenhouses with a shared dust filter and night lighting, a grow-water tank, a nutrient skid, three grow vaults, a spare hab blower, two hab ECLSS / CO2 scrub skids, two waste / water-recovery skids, two crew O2 buffers, a spare N2 tank farm, a visible water loop from two ice mines, an ice crush / feed depot with a covered crush bay and a parked ice-haul rover and a weigh station, ice-melt stills at mine 1 and mine 2, dust berms at both mines, a spare drill at mine 2, night lighting on the ice-haul road, a UV polish skid at the potable farm, a hab condensate return, and an ice/cargo haul road.
  • Survey and comms on the ice-rich northern plain (now two stake fields), a hab-street weather / dust mast, a spare inverter, a dust berm, and night lighting at the Earth dishes, plus two Earth-pointing dishes (light-time delay, not real-time; one is a backup).

See it. Walk it.

Live preview is a cinematic spectator of the current world. No controls — the camera tours the pad, habs, ISRU, and the empty next-window deck.

Walk is first-person on the surface. Click to lock the pointer, then WASD, mouse look, Shift to run, Space to jump. Spawn is on the landing-pad berm, looking at the Starships.

A world that changes

This is not a frozen screenshot. Structures, colliders, and landmarks land through ordinary git history. If you want a new hab, a less-janky Starship, or a better rover, open a PR. People walking tomorrow will see it.

Stay on the plan: cargo first, solar, ice, Sabatier, landing-surface prep, then habs. No backends, accounts, or analytics.
